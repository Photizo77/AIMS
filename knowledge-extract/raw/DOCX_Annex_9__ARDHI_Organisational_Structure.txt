About ARDHI
We are a Ugandan based NGO that drives community resilience through advocacy and systems innovations in the areas of agriculture and food security, waste management, disaster preparedness, and land governance.
Vision
A future where research informs action, and action delivers justice.

Mission
Impacting communities through research, innovations and advocacy particularly in areas of smart agriculture, waste management, disaster preparedness, health and land governance.

Our Values
Sustainability: Champion policies and actions that safeguard natural resources.
Equity: Foster inclusive environments and advocate for fair access to resources, ensuring marginalized voices are represented and empowered.
Innovation: Pioneer creative solutions and leverage emerging technologies to address complex systemic challenges.
Collaboration: Forge purposeful partnerships to amplify collective impact and drive lasting change.

Our Theory of Change
ARDHI is not a Think Tank, but a Think and Act Tank. Established in 2024 and fully registered in 2025, we operate on the belief that if we combine advocacy to shift rules, research to generate empirical evidence, and innovation to pilot practical models across agriculture, waste, disaster preparedness, health, and land governance, communities can gain the tools, voice, and systems they need to solve persistent problems. Lasting impact requires more than just ideas, it requires pressure, proof, and prototypes working together.
1. Purpose
This annex sets out how ARDHI Law and Policy Initiative is organised: who governs it, who manages it, who reports to whom, what each function is accountable for, and how a project team is staffed. It is written so that a funder can see that authority, delivery and assurance are properly separated, and so that ARDHI staff have a single reference for reporting lines and decision rights.
The organigram itself is presented in landscape at the end of this annex.
1.1 Design principles

Principle
 | How the structure reflects it
 | 
Separation of governance and management
 | The Board sets direction and holds management to account. No Board member holds an executive post or is employed by ARDHI.
 | 
Clear single line management
 | Every post has one line manager. Where a post also has a functional reporting line, that line is shown separately and is limited to technical matters.
 | 
Independent assurance
 | Safeguarding, internal audit and grievance handling have a reporting route that does not pass through the person who could be the subject of a concern.
 | 
Lean centre, strong field
 | Most posts sit in field delivery. Head office exists to support and to assure, not to duplicate.
 | 
Cross cutting responsibility is named
 | Safeguarding, inclusion, safeguards and data protection are held by named focal persons rather than left to everyone in general.
 | 
Community structures are part of the structure
 | Community paralegals, mediators and grievance contacts are shown on the chart because they deliver, and because they are what remains after a project ends.
 | 
Scalable
 | A new project adds a project team under the Programmes Manager without changing the organisational shape.
 | 

2. Governance Layer

Body
 | Composition
 | Meets
 | Decision rights
 | 
General Assembly or Members
 | Founding and admitted members
 | Annually
 | Appoints and removes Board members; receives the annual report and audited accounts; approves changes to the constitution
 | 
Board of Directors
 | Non executive members with a mix of legal, land, finance, research and community expertise; at least 40 percent women
 | At least quarterly
 | Approves strategy, annual plan and budget; approves policy; sets risk appetite; appoints the Country Director and Executive Director; approves any expenditure above the delegated ceiling
 |

 | At least three Board members, chaired by a member with financial expertise
 | Quarterly
 | Reviews budgets, management accounts, audit findings and fraud cases; recommends the auditor
 |

 | At least three Board members, including the Board Safeguarding Champion
 | Quarterly

 | 
Board Safeguarding Champion

Board members serve a fixed renewable term, sign a code of conduct and a conflict of interest declaration annually, and are not remunerated beyond reimbursement of reasonable costs. The Board carries out a self assessment once a year and records the actions arising from it.

 | 3.1 Why the Country Director sits above the Executive Director
ARDHI places the Country Director at the top of the executive line because accountability for what happens in Uganda, to Ugandan communities and before Ugandan regulators, sits in one place. The Executive Director leads strategy, partnerships and resource mobilisation and represents the organisation externally, working directly under  the Country Director and reporting to the same Board. Both posts are appointed by, and answerable to, the Board of Directors.

5. Cross Cutting Focal Persons
These roles are held by existing staff in addition to their substantive post, except where the size of the portfolio justifies a dedicated position. Each has a functional reporting line that bypasses ordinary line management, so that a concern can never be blocked by the person it concerns.

 | Functional reporting line
 | Core duties
 | 
Safeguarding Focal Person, and a deputy of the opposite sex
 | Two named staff, one female and one male
 | Executive Director, and directly to the Board Safeguarding Champion where the concern involves senior management
 | Receives and assesses concerns, maintains the case register, manages referral pathways, delivers training, prepares quarterly safeguarding reporting
 | 
Gender and Social Inclusion Focal Person
 | A named staff member
 | Country Director
 | GESI analysis, review of designs against the minimum standards, inclusion training, disaggregated performance reporting

 | 6. Delegation of Authority
Financial thresholds are expressed as bands and are set in shillings in the Finance Manual, then reviewed annually by the Board. No person may approve a payment from which they benefit, and no person may both authorise and execute the same payment.

 | Board of Directors

 | 8. Structure Governance

Item
 | Provision
 | 
Approval
 | The organisational structure is approved by the Board of Directors.
 | 
Review
 | Reviewed annually with the strategic plan, and whenever a major new programme starts or ends.
 | 
Change process
 | Any change to the structure is proposed by the Country Director, reviewed by the Programmes, Safeguarding and Risk Committee, and approved by the Board.
 | 
Job descriptions
 | Every post has a written job description with a reporting line, purpose, duties, competencies and safeguarding responsibilities. Job descriptions are reviewed at every appraisal.
